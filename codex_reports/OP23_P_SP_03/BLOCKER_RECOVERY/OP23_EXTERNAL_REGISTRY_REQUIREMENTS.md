## Historical/local-check review

- Requirements that match `StartDateTime` and/or `DocValidityDate` identify a specific prior/versioned record state, but no local authoritative history dataset or as-of lookup contract exists.
- Requirements referencing NMPТ registered before the Treaty explicitly require a separate legacy/pre-Treaty information resource. No authoritative local snapshot of that corpus exists.
- Rules that merely require `EndDateTime` to be absent still depend on the current external authoritative resource; the incoming XML alone cannot prove whether a matching active record exists or does not exist.
- Therefore none of the 39 can be reclassified as a purely local XML check from the available evidence.

## Closure evidence still required

For each registry family: an authoritative API/schema or dated offline snapshot, key definitions, active/history semantics, applicable version/effective date, and behavior for unavailable/stale/not-found data.
